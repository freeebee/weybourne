How brewing works

Every potion is judged on three things. Get all three right and you make a Masterwork.

1 · The balance

Each ingredient carries four essences — Ember, Tide, Verdant, Umbra. A formula wants them in a particular ratio, shown as white ticks on the balance bars. Match the ratio, not the amounts.

2 · The potency

Ingredients also carry potency, which simply adds up. The formula names a target — overshoot and the draught is harsh, undershoot and it's watery. A little Spring Water shifts balance without adding much potency; Crystal Dust adds potency without shifting balance.

3 · The infusion

Hold the heat inside the formula's green band and infusion fills — fastest dead centre. Outside the band it slips backwards. At 100% the brew is ready; every second past that scorches it, so bottle promptly.

Turbulence

Hot cauldrons churn, and every ingredient you drop in churns them more. Stir to settle it. Let turbulence reach 100% and the pot boils over — you lose infusion and the mark stays on the potion.

The day

  • Commissions expire. Miss one and your standing suffers.
  • Closing shop costs 6 coin in coal and candles, restocks the market, and brings new custom.
  • Standing unlocks better formulas — and better-paying clients.
  • Anything on the shelf can be sold to a passing pedlar for about half what a commission would pay.
